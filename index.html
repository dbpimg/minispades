How to Play MiniSpades

You are South. North is your computer teammate; East and West are your opponents. Each hand deals 13 cards per seat. The deck replaces 2♣ and 2♥ with Little Joker and High Joker. The 2♦ is a trump. Trump order: High Joker > Little Joker > 2♦ > 2♠ > A♠ down to 3♠.

Bid: select how many tricks you expect to win. Bid zero (Nil) to aim for no tricks. The computers bid automatically.

Play: choose a highlighted legal card. Follow the led suit when you can. Spades are trump and may be led after they have been broken (or if you have only spades). The highest trump wins, or the highest card of the led suit when no trump is played.

Score: reach your partnership bid to earn 10 points per bid trick plus overtricks; missing the bid costs 10 points per bid trick. Successful Nil adds 100 points; failed Nil subtracts 100. Every 10 bags costs 100 points. Play toward 500 points.
